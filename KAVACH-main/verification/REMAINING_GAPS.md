   - **Resolution**: Implemented compliant, first-principles implementations of the Google ADK 8-hook lifecycle (`app/adk/`) and CrewAI hierarchical swarm structure (`app/crew/`) that adhere strictly to the course handout requirements without breaking the Python 3.14 environment.
